  WILDCARD COVERAGE NOTE (read first)
========================================================================
  A firewall wildcard "*.foo.com" matches ONE label to the left (x.foo.com)
  but usually NOT a deeper label (x.y.foo.com) and NOT the apex (foo.com).
  Several gaps below exist purely because of that depth/apex rule. If the
  network team's device does prefix-suffix contains-matching instead of
  strict single-label wildcards, the "DEPTH" items may already be covered -
  confirm with them rather than assuming.
